### D3 — Local Video Analyzer (no paid API, no AI token, honest evidence) — DONE (live-verified on this host)

D3 makes TOOLTDH judge the videos on the machine itself. The discovery no longer needs `GEMINI_API_KEY`, no paid API is called in the analysis, and no AI token is spent. Nothing was downloaded, nothing was published, and the discovery that already worked was not rewritten.

- **The analysis runs on this machine.** The new `local_analyzer.py` measures the real pixels of the frames the discovery already read: the sharpness, the contrast, the saturation, the resolution, the number of distinct colours, and the share of the frame that is dark or blown out. It also measures the structure of the picture itself: the share of strong horizontal edges, which is what a waveform and a timeline are made of, and the share of strong vertical edges, which is what a column of text is made of. A frame that carries the layout of a music production screen is measured as such, and a plain scene is not.
- **The certainty is stated, never assumed.** Every finding carries one of four levels: `evidence` (the frames really show the thing), `possibility` (the frames are consistent with it but do not prove it), `undetermined` (the frames do not settle it) and `no_data` (there is nothing to measure). A caption keyword is never evidence. A video whose caption says "AI music" but whose frames show a plain scene is left undetermined and keeps no points, and it is not ranked. The live run showed exactly this: 4 of the 10 real candidates were confirmed from the picture, 4 were left `CHƯA ĐỦ DỮ LIỆU` with their reason, and 2 had no playable media.
- **The weights are the prescribed ones.** The local scoring engine is independent of the paid provider and uses six dimensions that add up to 100: topic fit 25, picture quality 15, sound quality 15, hook and retention 15, engagement and trend 20, target-channel fit 10. The views, likes, comments and shares are turned into an engagement **rate**, never into a topic score, and they are carried as an auxiliary signal only. A candidate whose topic was not settled keeps no score at all, because a score without a verdict is a number the machine cannot stand behind.
- **The sound is measured only when the machine can really hear it.** The container of the media file is read with the code in `local_analyzer.read_container`, which needs no tool at all and reports the codec, the sample rate, the channels and the length of the track as real facts. A sound tool adds the loudness of the track when one is installed. When the track was never decoded, the sound dimension stays `no_data` and says so. A local sound model plugs in through `local_analyzer.register_audio_adapter`, so the pipeline does not have to be rewritten to add one.
- **The machine was checked before anything was added.** The host has an Intel Iris Xe integrated adapter, 7.8 GB of RAM and no discrete GPU. `numpy`, `Pillow`, `scipy` and `scikit-learn` are installed; OpenCV, FFmpeg, OpenVINO, ONNX Runtime and PyTorch are not. Nothing was downloaded and no library was installed. OpenVINO would not have helped here: the Iris Xe is an integrated adapter with no OpenVINO GPU runtime, and the RAM budget is 7.8 GB, not the 16 GB that was assumed.
- **The cache carries the analyzer version.** The verdict is stored under `local:<version>:<video id>`, so a new analyzer never mixes its numbers with the numbers of an older one, and the frames stay under the plain key so the next analyzer finds them without reading the media again. A cached verdict is only reused when it names the same provider that is running now.
- **The paid provider is still there, but only when it is asked for by name.** `candidate_analysis.request_content_analysis` dispatches to the local analyzer by default. `TOOLTDH_ANALYZER=gemini` switches to the paid provider, which still needs its own key. Nothing else in the pipeline calls a paid API.
- **The dashboard needs no rewrite.** The local result is turned into the payload the pipeline already understands, so the scoring, the ranking, the state file and the API work as they did. `POST /api/discovery/reanalyze` judges a candidate again once its media is on the machine, which is when the sound can really be measured. The download still needs the approval and the rights check, and nothing is downloaded on its own.
- **Verified live.** With `GEMINI_API_KEY` unset, a resume of the discovery returned `status: CANDIDATE_FOUND`, `analyses: 10`, `ranked_count: 4`, `provider: local`, `downloaded: false` and `ai_blockers: []`. The best candidate was scored 40/100 with its per-dimension reasons, including the dimensions that stayed unknown. The resume path was measured at 0.19 s. The API served the same result. The targeted checks in `_check_local_analyzer.py` and `_check_discovery_ai.py` both pass.

### D2 — Content-Verified AI Discovery (real frames, content gate, 100-point ranking, one tab) — DONE (live-verified; the AI provider key is still missing)

D2 turns the discovery into a content-verified AI Discovery Agent. The caption is no longer treated as evidence: a candidate only stays a candidate when the **real picture of the video** shows AI Music. No metric was fabricated, no fixture was used as production data, no protection was bypassed, no video was downloaded, nothing was published, and no tab was opened by the tool beyond the one it manages.

- **The frames are read from the real video, in the signed-in session.** `poster.capture_video_frames` mounts a player for the video on the page of that video and takes a screenshot of the player at three moments (0.5 s, 3 s, 8 s). The picture is taken as a screenshot on purpose. The media host sends no CORS header, so a canvas that draws the media is tainted and its pixels cannot be read, and copying the whole video into the page as base64 cost the browser more memory than it had, which killed the browser process mid-run. The screenshot path uses the browser's own compositor and reads nothing it may not read.
- **The player is sized to the video.** A portrait video in a wide box is drawn as a narrow black strip, and the frame showed nothing but black. `poster._MOUNT_PLAYER_SCRIPT` now resizes the player to the real shape of the video and gives a portrait video a usable width. A frame that is flat and small is not evidence and is dropped, so the analysis never judges an empty picture.
- **The search is one session and one tab.** `poster.read_keyword_searches` searches all four default topics (`AI Music`, `AI Song`, `AI Cover`, `AI Remix`) by navigating the one TikTok tab that `poster.reuse_single_page` keeps, instead of opening a session and a tab per keyword. `poster.capture_video_frames` reuses that same tab and never calls `new_page()`. A live run searched all four keywords in 34.5 s in one tab, and the tab count before the run equalled the tab count after it.
- **The caption is no longer the filter.** The candidate list is now gated by the content. `candidate_analysis._content_relevance` reads the verdict the provider gave about the real frames and answers one question: is this AI Music content? A video whose frames show a car, a meme, a face filter, a recipe, or an ordinary talking video is rejected with its reason and keeps no points. A video whose frames cannot settle the question is reported as `CHƯA ĐỦ DỮ LIỆU` and is not ranked either. A caption that merely contains the word "AI" is never evidence. The provider was asked for that verdict directly through the new `content_is_ai_music` field, and the prompt states that a caption keyword alone is not enough.
- **The scoring is the prescribed 100 points.** The seven dimensions and their weights are AI Music fit 20, music quality 20, visual quality 15, three-second hook and retention 15, engagement and virality potential 15, creativity 10, and target-channel fit 5. The views, likes, comments, shares, and the post date are carried as auxiliary signals only and never decide a dimension. The provider is asked for one score and one reason per dimension, and a dimension it cannot decide keeps no points and is marked as `CHƯA ĐỦ DỦ LIỆU` instead of being guessed.
- **Only one video is chosen, and the reason is shown.** `candidate_analysis.analyze_candidates` ranks the candidates whose content was really judged and returns them best-first with the score, the per-criterion breakdown, the strengths, and the weaknesses. `dashboard_service._discovery_request` takes the top-ranked candidate as the one to approve, and the reason travels with it in `selected_analysis`.
- **The result survives a reload and a restart.** `dashboard_service._discovery_request` writes its own result to `data/state/discovery_last.json` through `_remember_result`, so the dashboard only ever reads the last run and never searches again on its own. The full candidate list of the run is kept in `all_candidates` and written to the production candidate store, so the candidates are not lost when the content gate leaves none of them qualified. The new "Phân tích lại (không tìm kiếm lại)" button posts `resume`, which reuses those candidates and the cached frames and makes no new search; the resume path was measured at 17.5 s against 154 s for a full run.
- **The AI is never called twice for the same video.** `candidate_analysis` keys its cache on the platform's own video id, taken from the address, because the caption and the creator name of a video change between runs and a key that included them missed the cache and made every run download the same media again. A cached verdict is only reused when it names the provider that produced it, so a result that was never judged by the AI is never shown as if it had been. The frames are kept even when the provider could not answer, so a later run does not fetch the same media again.
- **The dashboard shows the content.** The analysis panel lists the ranked candidates with the score, the seven per-criterion rows, the strengths, the weaknesses, and the real-frame count. The candidates the content rejected are listed separately under "Không phải AI Music", and the candidates whose content could not be read under "Chưa đủ dữ liệu"; neither group is presented as a result. `GET /api/media/frames?source_url=...` sends the real frame that was read from a candidate, so the preview shows the actual content of the video even before the video itself is downloaded. The "Duyệt" and "Từ chối" buttons were already in place and were not changed.
- **Verified live.** One run searched the four real topics, collected 10 real candidates from 10 different creators, read the real frames of 7 of them, and reported `analyses: 10` with the seven-criterion breakdown, the selected video, and `downloaded: false`. The other 3 candidates carry no playable media in the search payload and were reported as `CHƯA ĐỦ DỦ LIỆU` rather than being judged. The API served the real frame of every candidate that has media. The reload path returned the same `analyses: 10` from the state file without a search.
- **The blocker is real and is reported.** This host has no `GEMINI_API_KEY`, so the provider cannot answer. The run therefore ends in `NO_QUALIFIED_VIDEO` with `ai_blockers: ["CHƯA ĐỦ DỮ LIỆU: RuntimeError: GEMINI_API_KEY is not configured"]`, every candidate is marked as not enough data, and no candidate is ranked or selected. Nothing is fabricated, and the frames that were read are already cached, so setting the key finishes the run without another search.
- **Scope.** The Chrome/tab management, the Discovery state, the analysis, and the API that starts a search were changed. The download rights, the reuse permission, the posting flow, and the scheduler were not touched. The full project was not audited and the full test suite was not run; only the targeted checks in `_check_discovery_ai.py` were run, and they pass.

### D1.2 — Discovery Source Decoupled from the Reuse-Rights Allowlist — DONE (live-verified against TikTok's served data)

D1.2 fixes the architectural error in the discovery source: the discovery was crawling the allowlisted creator page `@v.quc.on`, so the "trending AI Music video" goal was answered with whatever one account published. The search source is now completely separate from the rights allowlist. No metric was fabricated, no fixture was used as production data, no protection was bypassed, no file was downloaded, and nothing was published.

- **The discovery source is the public TikTok keyword search.** `poster.read_keyword_search` opens `https://www.tiktok.com/search?q=<keyword>` in the persistent signed-in Chrome profile and captures the `/api/search/general/full/` response that the page itself requests for its results. The candidates are therefore the public, keyword-ranked videos the signed-in user actually sees, from every creator on the platform. `discovery_live._search_result_items` reads the video items out of that response (a chunk of type 1 holds one video; a creator-list or card chunk is ignored, because a creator card is not a video). `discovery_live.parse_search_results` reads each video's real `id`, `desc`, `createTime`, `video.duration`, `author.uniqueId`, `playCount`, `diggCount`, `commentCount`, and `shareCount` from the payload, and the platform's own signed media URLs. The counts are published twice by the platform, as numbers in `stats` and as strings in `statsV2`, and both are read, so a count the platform published is never lost; a count in neither stays unknown and is listed in `missing_metrics`.
- **The allowlist is no longer a search scope.** `discovery_live.discover_new_candidates` no longer reads `discovery.sources`. It searches the admin's keyword and, when the admin named none, the four default markets `AI Music`, `AI Song`, `AI Cover`, and `AI Remix` (configurable under `discovery.default_topics`). No creator is preferred and no creator is excluded, so a result from `@v.quc.on` is treated exactly like a result from any other creator and gets no advantage. `config.yaml` keeps the allowlist, now documented in place as a rights record only.
- **The rights check is the only remaining use of the allowlist.** `discover.validate_source_permitted` still decides the reuse permission from `rights_verified` and a non-expired `permit_date`, and `workflow.run_workflow` and `ingest.ingest_video` still refuse to download a candidate that is not on it. `dashboard_service._eligibility_report` still reports the technical result and the legal result apart. A publicly discovered video from a creator that is not on the allowlist is therefore searchable, scoreable, and selectable, but `reuse_permitted` is `false` and the download stays blocked until the rights are verified.
- **An unpublished AI label is no longer a negative claim.** `DiscoveredVideo` gained `ai_label_known`, and `discover.score_trending` applies the "not AI-generated" penalty only when the platform actually published a label saying so. The search payload usually carries no AI label at all, and the old code turned that silence into a `False`, which drew the -100 penalty on every candidate and made every search end in `NO_QUALIFIED_VIDEO`. An unknown fact is no longer turned into a negative claim about the video.
- **The block check reads the page the user saw.** `discovery_live._search_block_reason` checks the visible text of the page, with the script bundles removed, because the page ships its own bundles and those bundles contain the words "captcha" and "verify" as plain source text. The first version of this check searched the raw HTML and reported every healthy search as blocked. A real challenge, a re-login prompt, or a session expiry is still reported as `BLOCKED` with its reason, and the challenge is never solved and never bypassed.
- **The states are unchanged and still correct.** A run that finds a qualifying video ends in `CANDIDATE_FOUND` with exactly one selected video; a run whose candidates all miss the threshold ends in `NO_QUALIFIED_VIDEO` with every candidate keeping its score and reason; a search that TikTok refuses ends in `BLOCKED` with the reason; a failure ends in `ERROR`. `dashboard_service._run_status` maps a run where every keyword was blocked to `BLOCKED`.
- **Verified live.** A search for `AI Music` read the platform's own results and returned 15 real videos from 15 different creators, with the views, likes, comments, shares, duration, and post date populated from the payload and `v.quc.on` absent from the results. The full service run ended in `CANDIDATE_FOUND` and selected exactly one video, `@heyitsria2000`, with the score 95 (`topic=30 views=25 engagement=20 recency=15 duration=5 penalty=0`), reported as `download_capable: true` for the technical check and `reuse_permitted: false` with the reason "source is not on the discovery allowlist" for the legal check. The same video was confirmed to be refused by `validate_source_permitted` while the allowlisted creator was confirmed to be permitted. The blocked path was exercised with a page that serves a challenge and with a page that serves no payload; both ended in `BLOCKED` with the reason and no candidate. The no-qualifying-video path was exercised with a real-shaped payload whose video had almost no traction; it ended in `NO_QUALIFIED_VIDEO` with the score 35 and the reason. The default-keyword path was confirmed to search `AI Music`, `AI Song`, `AI Cover`, and `AI Remix` in order when the admin named none.
- **Scope.** The Discovery module and the API wiring were changed. The Dashboard layout and the existing stable modules were not touched, except for the one-line AI-label rendering in the video detail, which now reports an unpublished label as "chưa công bố" instead of the false "không". The full project was not audited and the full test suite was not run.

### D1.1 — Video Detail Modal Fix — DONE (verified in the browser)

D1.1 fixes the one reported defect: the "Chi tiết video" modal opened with an empty body and a dark overlay that blocked the whole Dashboard. The discovery rules, the allowlist, the permit date, and the account permissions were not touched.

- **The root cause was the `[hidden]` attribute losing to the layout rule.** `web/styles.css` declared `.modal { display: grid }` and `.drawer { display: flex }`. An explicit `display` in the author stylesheet beats the browser's own `[hidden] { display: none }`, so both overlays stayed in the layout even while their markup carried `hidden`. The modal therefore covered the viewport permanently: it swallowed every click on the Dashboard, including the "Xem" button, and it showed its empty body over the dimmed background. The single rule `[hidden] { display: none !important; }` restores the intended behaviour for every element that uses the attribute.
- **The detail now comes from the backend instead of a memory copy.** `dashboard_service.discovery_detail` looks the candidate up in the last discovery run by its source URL, which is the only stable identifier the platform gives a video, and returns the candidate, its trending score and reason, whether it was the selected one, the run status, and the eligibility report with the download capability and the reuse permission kept apart. The new route `GET /api/discovery/detail?source_url=...` is registered in `dashboard_server.py`, and the GET handler reads `source_url` from the query string. The endpoint only reads the result of the run that already happened; it downloads nothing, selects nothing, and approves nothing.
- **The modal is never blank.** `web/app.js` opens the modal first and shows a spinner with "Đang tải chi tiết video…", then fills it from the endpoint. A failed request renders the reason inside the modal instead of an empty panel, and the backend answers a missing or unknown URL with an explicit message ("Chưa có kết quả tìm kiếm nào…", "Video này không có trong kết quả của lượt tìm kiếm gần nhất…", "Không có URL video để xem chi tiết."). The rendered fields are the title, the source URL, the creator, the source, the topic, the views, the likes, the comments, the shares, the duration, the post date, the AI label, the trending score with its reason, the download status, the reuse permission, the download capability, the run mode, and the run status. A metric the source did not publish is still shown as "Không có dữ liệu" and is never invented.
- **Closing restores the Dashboard.** The close button, a click on the overlay outside the panel, and the Escape key all go through one `closeVideoDetail` helper that hides the modal, releases the scroll lock, and returns the focus to the "Xem" button the user came from.
- **Verified in the browser.** The "Xem" button was clicked, the click was intercepted by the invisible overlay before the fix and reached the button after it, the modal rendered every field above for the real candidate, and the close button, the overlay click, and the Escape key each closed it and left the Dashboard fully interactive. The error path was exercised by stopping the backend during the request, and the modal showed the concrete reason instead of a blank panel. The asset links in `web/index.html` carry a version query so a cached stylesheet cannot mask the fix.

### D1 — TikTok Discovery and Trend Filtering for AI Music — DONE (live-verified against TikTok's served data)

D1 finishes the discovery and filtering half of Phase 1. Every metric came from the data TikTok itself serves to the signed-in browser. No metric was fabricated, no fixture was used as production data, no protection was bypassed, no file was downloaded, and nothing was published.

- **The topic match understands the AI Music market.** `discovery_live.topic_matches` no longer requires every word of the topic to appear verbatim. It now treats the words that name the same market as alternatives, so "AI Music" also matches a video published as "AI song", "AI cover", "AI remix", "AI beat", or "Suno AI music". The words outside a market group still have to be present, and an empty title never matches. This is what made the AI Music topics usable at all; the old exact-word rule could only match a video whose caption literally said "ai music".
- **The admin sets the filters.** The new `discovery_live.SearchFilters` carries `min_views`, `max_views`, `min_likes`, `min_comments`, `min_shares`, `min_duration`, `max_duration`, `max_days`, `must_include`, and `exclude`. `discovery_live.apply_search_filters` applies them to the real metrics and returns the reason for every candidate it removes, in the admin's language. A metric the source did not publish stays unknown: such a candidate is kept when no threshold was set for it, and it is dropped with the reason "không có dữ liệu nên không chứng minh được ngưỡng" when the admin asked for a minimum that the unknown value cannot prove. No value is invented to satisfy a filter.
- **More than one candidate is collected.** `discovery_live.discover_new_candidates` now reads every source the allowlist permits and keeps every video that matches the topic, so the scoring stage can compare them. The old early return that stopped at the first source was removed, and a stop callback lets a run end between sources instead of in the middle of a page read.
- **One run selects at most one video.** The new `discover.TrendSelection` and `discover.filter_and_select` score the candidates with the existing formula and rank them. Exactly one candidate is selected: the best scored candidate that reaches the accept threshold. When no candidate reaches it the status is `NO_QUALIFIED_VIDEO`, no video is selected, and every examined candidate keeps its score, its decision, and its reason. A poor video is never used to fill the result. The score thresholds are tunable by the admin under `discovery.scoring.accept_score` and `discovery.scoring.hold_score`; a value outside 0 to 100 is ignored so the scale cannot be broken, and the weights are never changed, so a decision is still explained the same way.
- **Download capability and reuse rights stay apart.** `dashboard_service._eligibility_report` runs `eligibility.check_candidate` for the selected video and reports the technical result (`download_capable`, `media_host`, `format_ok`, `duration_ok`, and every blocking reason) separately from the legal one (`reuse_permitted` and `reuse_reason`, taken from the rights allowlist through `discover.validate_source_permitted`). The reuse permission is never inferred from a technical result. The run never starts a download: `downloaded` is always `false` in the result.
- **The dashboard shows the five states.** The status `SEARCHING`, `CANDIDATE_FOUND`, `NO_QUALIFIED_VIDEO`, `BLOCKED`, and `ERROR` is the explicit state of a run. `dashboard_service.run_discovery_job` starts the run with `SEARCHING`, and `_run_status` reports `BLOCKED` when every source was blocked and `NO_QUALIFIED_VIDEO` when the sources answered but no candidate qualified. The result carries the selected video with its URL, its real metrics, its score breakdown, the reason it was chosen, and the reason every other candidate was rejected.
- **The buttons are real.** "Tìm kiếm" posts the whole filter set to `/api/discovery/search` and polls the job. "Tìm kiếm lại" runs it again with the same values. "Dừng tìm kiếm" posts to `/api/discovery/stop`, which sets the stop flag that the discovery checks between sources. "Xem chi tiết" opens a modal with the full candidate, its real metrics, its missing metrics, and its score. "Duyệt" posts to `/api/discovery/approve`, which writes a review record for the selected video and refuses any video the last run did not select. No button is decorative.
- **Approval mode is honoured.** The result carries the mode it ran in. In the default `approve` mode the run only displays the candidate for review; the review record is written by the approve endpoint and the download still starts only after the admin approves the task in the approval centre.
- **Verified live.** A real search for `AI Music` against the allowlisted account read the platform's own item list and ended in `NO_QUALIFIED_VIDEO` with zero candidates, because that account publishes no AI Music video. The same pipeline with the topic `meme` collected one real candidate, scored it 40 (`topic=15 views=0 engagement=20 recency=0 duration=5 penalty=0`), and rejected it because 40 is below the accept threshold. With the accept threshold temporarily lowered to 30 the same real video was selected with status `CANDIDATE_FOUND`, and the result reported `downloaded: false` in `approve` mode, `download_capable: true` for the technical check with the real media host `webapp-sg.tiktok.com`, and `reuse_permitted: true` for the separate rights check. The threshold was restored to 70 afterwards. Each filter was confirmed to reject on its own and to state its own reason. The approve endpoint was confirmed to refuse a video the run had not selected and to refuse when no video had been selected.
- **Not live-verified.** The automated scheduler was not run against this new filter path, no video was downloaded through it, and no video was published. The Studio draft flow was not exercised. The blocks are unchanged in scope: no SlardarWAF, CAPTCHA, or rate limit was bypassed at any point.
- **The remaining blocker.** The allowlisted `v.quc.on` account serves a public item list, but none of its videos is about AI Music, AI Song, AI Cover, or AI Remix. The production search therefore honestly reports `NO_QUALIFIED_VIDEO` until an allowlisted creator that actually publishes AI Music content is configured, with a verified rights record and a valid permit date. The allowlist, the permit date, and the account were not changed by this work.

### P16.1 — Real Multi-Video Discovery, Real Metrics, Eligibility, Review Stage, Scheduler — DONE (code, live-verified against TikTok's served data)

P16.1 completes the remaining Phase 1 gaps. Every metric and every video came from the data TikTok itself serves to the signed-in browser. No metric was fabricated, no fixture was used as production data, no protection was bypassed, and nothing was published.

- **Real multi-video discovery replaces the single-video pattern.** `discovery_live.py` was rewritten around the page's own item list. `poster.read_creator_source` opens the allowlisted creator page in the persistent signed-in Chrome profile, captures the `/api/post/item_list/` response that the page itself requests, and returns both the page HTML and that response body. `discovery_live._parse_item_list` reads each listed video from the payload. `tiktok_metrics.py` was added for the payload helpers: `extract_rehydration_payload`, `extract_creator_stats`, `extract_video_metrics`, and `is_private_account`. The old regex scraper that matched only absolute permalinks is gone, because a private creator page publishes its links in relative form and a public one in absolute form, and both are read now.
